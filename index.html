Mud · Bamboo · Lime

Built by Hand, on Site

No concrete shell, no cladding over the top. Every home is raised the slow way — earth blocks laid by hand, bamboo bent and lashed on site, thatch layered roof by roof.

Earth blocks, laid by hand

Earth blocks go up course by course. The walls breathe, and their thermal mass holds the heat outside where it belongs.

Bamboo, bent and lashed

Treated bamboo is curved into gridshells and tied joint by joint — structure and ceiling in the same gesture.

Thatch, layered roof by roof

The domes are clad in woven panels and thatch — a roof that sheds monsoon rain and stays quiet in the heat.
