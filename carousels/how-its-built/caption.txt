Mud, bamboo, lime. That's the whole list.

Earth blocks, laid by hand — the ground the house stands on becomes the house.
A bamboo grid shell, bent and lashed, with no steel frame. Thatch laid in
courses, the way it has always been laid. Inside, earth plaster shaped and
burnished by hand.

Then it becomes a home. Cool in the heat. Quiet in the rain. Made of the place
it stands in.

See it being built.
