## What I'm buying

**1. What exactly am I buying — the land, the house, or both?**

is built on it at a per-square-foot rate. The home is designed and built to
order — there is no fixed model to pick from, so the size and the layout are
yours to set. You can see both lines in the breakdown on the Holiday Homes
page.

on it, not land alone. It is the smallest plot we sell (726 sq yds) with a
1 BHK on it, and it is the first row of the table below.

> **NEEDS YOUR ANSWER — and please check this one with your counsel before we
> publish anything.** Under FEMA, a non-resident cannot *purchase* agricultural
